The artist

Quietly uncompromising.

Raised in Croydon on British radio, R&B and UK garage, Sarah Lamera spent her twenties recording demos between ordinary jobs. During the quiet breakdown of a long-term relationship, candid voice notes began to turn into songs.

Now beginning her independent career at 38, Sarah writes with dry humour and emotional precision, finding tension in everyday conversations, unfinished sentences, and the ordinary details people remember.
