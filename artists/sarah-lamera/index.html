The artist

Quietly uncompromising.

Raised in Croydon on British radio, R&B and UK garage, Sarah spent her twenties working ordinary jobs and recording demos. During the quiet breakdown of a long-term relationship, candid voice notes began to turn into songs.

Now launching an independent career at 38, Sarah writes in lived-in details, dry humour and restrained honesty. Her music finds its turning points in unfinished conversations and ordinary evenings.

Her sound

Understated.
Nocturnal.
Human.

Deep dry electronic drums, warm sub-bass, sparse felt piano and detuned analogue synths. Sarah's warm, slightly husky low-mezzo voice keeps the emotional centre close and conversational.

Her debut era, The Things We Don't Say, explores how familiar rooms and small domestic details hold the words people can no longer find.
